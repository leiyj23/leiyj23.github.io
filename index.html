Yijia Lei | 雷一嘉

I am a fourth-year undergraduate student at Tsinghua University, double majoring in Fundamental Science (Mathematics & Physics) and Mechanical Engineering.

Currently, I collaborate with Prof. Ehsan Adeli, Yao He and Juze Zhang at Stanford University, under Prof. Adeli's supervision.

I am also a research intern at MeowLab, College of AI, Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Miao Liu, and at Starwith AI, advised by Prof. Yao Feng. I also conduct research at ARL, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Tsinghua University, where I was supervised by Prof. Yao Jiang.

My research interests lie in human-centered robot learning. I am particularly interested in how robots can acquire versatile capabilities by learning from humans and the physical world, and how they can understand, reason, act, and collaborate with people in dynamic environments.

Publications and Manuscripts

EgoSAT: A Comprehensive Benchmark of Egocentric Streaming Interaction Understanding

Yijia Lei, Jinzhao Li, Yichi Zhang, Jiacheng Hua, Yin Li, Miao Liu

ECCV 2026 · Accepted

Paper / Project Page / Code

A comprehensive benchmark for egocentric streaming interaction understanding, unifying retrospective, present, and prospective reasoning under causal observation constraints. It also examines future interaction predictability, model confidence, and answerability.

IPIBench: Evaluating Interactive Proactive Intelligence of MLLMs under Continuous Streams

Jinzhao Li, Yinuo Chen, Wenxuan Song, Yijia Lei, Yichi Zhang, Honglei Yan, Panwang Pan, Miao Liu

NeurIPS 2026, Evaluations & Datasets Track · Accepted

Paper / Project Page

A benchmark for proactive intelligence in continuous video streams, evaluating when multimodal models should respond, how they manage evolving user requests, and how they coordinate reactive and proactive interactions.
